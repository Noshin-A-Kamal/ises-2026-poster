Consumer Product Exposure Assessment

Mapping Activity-Specific Data Gaps in Consumer Product Exposure Assessment

Noshin Anjum Kamal¹ · Ajaya Kumar Sahoo² · Gaeul Yang² · Jinhee Choi² · Hyeong-Moo Shin¹

¹ School of Earth and Environmental Sciences, Baylor University, Waco, Texas, USA

² School of Environmental Engineering, University of Seoul, Seoul, Republic of Korea

Chemical-level exposure estimates do not always provide sufficient information for activity-specific assessment.

Poster overview

This study maps activity-specific supporting-information gaps across a large consumer-product chemical inventory and distinguishes the availability of a chemical-level exposure estimate from the information needed to assess a specific use activity.

8,009
chemical × activity pairs evaluated
3,361
pairs matched to existing consumer exposure estimates
1,034
matched pairs that were fully model-ready
518 / 877
pairs requiring additional input information that met the model-specific screening level in at least one model
